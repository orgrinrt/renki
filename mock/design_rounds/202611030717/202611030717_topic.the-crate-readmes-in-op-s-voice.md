# the-crate-readmes-in-op-s-voice

Op, 3 November 2026, quoted whole: "Overall most of the readmes just explain too much and too serious guy role plays a chill guy trying to speak casually that reads nerdy and embarrassing, so perhaps just tone that shit down. And remove the bullet lists and excess tables in all of the readmes." And then: "Use my voice and be casual but don't be embarrassing or clearly LLM is what I'm saying. And don't over explain or write up personal memories or history in short to explain why a tool exists".

The intent is that each published crate's readme is short, in his voice, with no list, no excess table and no backstory, and that every published crate has its own readme. `renki` was pointing at the repository root file, so it gets a readme of its own in its directory, carrying what the root one carried, cut down. The root readme becomes a short page naming the three crates. `renki-config` and `renki-dirs` are cut down in the same way.
