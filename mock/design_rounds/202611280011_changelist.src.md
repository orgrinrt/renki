## Comparison to deprecated changelist

### comparison to deprecated changelist

The deprecated changelist kept the `renki-config` half written under the first
source window and claimed a `renki-manifest` reading into a fixed
`Room<TEXT, NODES>`. What it kept stands: `layer_of`, `Part`, `LayerName`,
`BadLayer`, `Order` with `Order::DEFAULT` and `Order::rank`, `Meta` with
`Meta::defect`, `Shape`, `Meet`, `BadMerge`, `Layer`, `ListRun`,
`Predicate`, `Predicates`, `Answers`, `BadPredicate`, `PredicateName`,
`Declared::named`, `Store::shape`, and the layer in `ResolvedView` and
`BadConfig`. The host, `Host` and `Unix`, with `BadRead` and the writer's
fixtures stand as committed. What changes is what met the store contract: the
`Room` borrows its memory and carries the decode and path shelves, the size in

`Toml`, `Manifest` and `Ambient` are built over `tomlfuse-parse`'s document
once the stack's string and number crates are on the registry.
