# doc changelist

Manifest layers, the `meta` table, named predicates, the crate that reads a
manifest off the disk without `std`, the stack's own TOML parser in its place
of two TOML crates, the POSIX reader, and the tool descriptor's new fields.
Descends from the locked round 202609041058, whose contracts it extends and
keeps `no_std`. Consumes the four topics of this round whole:

- `202611272025-b9a2_topic.where-the-manifest-reading-lives.md`: a new crate,
  `renki-manifest`, does the i/o; the rules stay in `renki-config`; the TOML
  store moves; the descriptor gains `who`, `moment` and `settings`. Its
  choice of a `std` crate is reopened and replaced by the fourth topic.
- `202611272025-b9a2_topic.layers-meta-and-predicates.md`: which files are
  layers, the order, the `meta` keys, includes to a fixed point, the merge,
  tracked and untracked layers, the predicate, the provenance.
- `202611272025-b9a2_topic.the-posix-reader.md`: written by hand, a stated
  TOML subset refused outside, one dump format, one fixture suite both
  readers run, everything under `posix/`.
- `202611272025-b9a2_topic.reading-without-std.md`: `renki-manifest` is
  `#![no_std]`, no alloc, and passes the lints as they stand; its i/o is a
  `Host` over the C library per unix target; the caller gives the room; TOML
  is read by `tomlfuse-parse`, a crate of its own in the tomlfuse repository,
  held to toml-test; renki drops `toml` and `toml_edit`. It rests on the
  sketches `202611272130_no-std-file-reading-on-unix` (WORKS) and
  `202611272150_a-no-std-toml-parser-against-toml-test` (WORKS: 1.0.0 valid
  208/208 and invalid 501/501, 1.1.0 valid 218/218 and invalid 494/494).

No other flat topic is in `mock/design_rounds/`, and no remote branch carries
one.

## Outside this repository, and its order

- `tomlfuse-parse`, a new crate in the tomlfuse repository beside
  `tomlfuse-core`: `#![no_std]`, no alloc, stable, naming no user, from the
  sketch's draft, with toml-test's valid and invalid cases for every spec
  version the data carries run as its tests. It lands on tomlfuse's `dev` and
  is published to the registry before renki's next release candidate, since
  a git dependency keeps renki from publishing. Until it is published,
  renki's source names it by a path or git dependency on its work branch and
  is not released. At the second reopening it keeps every node it reads, with
  its key's span and its value's span, and offers a read view over the filled
  index: a lookup by dotted path, a node's children, the elements of an array
  of tables. At the ninth reopening it takes its index through
  `notko::lend::Lend` and refuses a short one with `ErrorKind::Exhausted`,
  carrying notko's `Exhausted`.
- The bare-type lints, where they live: at the ninth reopening, they read
  only the crates that take arvo; as they landed, each reads a crate that
  takes the crate of its replacement, so those that replace `Result` and
  `Option` read `renki-manifest`, which takes notko. The skip of an impl of another crate's trait
  this list named before is withdrawn; an implementer wraps the bare types such
  a signature fixes behind a trait or newtype of its own.
- The stack's string and number crates on the registry. `renki-config`
  publishes, so every crate it depends on must be on the registry at a version
  it can publish against; neither the string crate nor the number crate is
  there now. Until they are, `renki-config`'s port off bare types cannot depend
  on them. `renki-manifest` takes no arvo and does not wait on them.
