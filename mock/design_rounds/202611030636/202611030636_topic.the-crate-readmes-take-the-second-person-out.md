# the-crate-readmes-take-the-second-person-out


Op's words, 3 November, quoted whole: "Queue up a sub agent of yours to update
READMEs across the crates that we use and have contributed to upstream". The
intent in them is that the published prose of each repo is current and in his
voice, with no second person in a public document.

The readmes of `renki-config` and `renki-dirs` are doc templates to the gate,
so a round opens for them. Three changes: the `Support` section of each gains
the contribution paragraph the other readmes of the estate carry, the licence
block of `renki-config` takes the same order as the others, and its
limitations line no longer speaks of a column its table does not have. In
`renki-dirs` the motivation no longer retells how a cleanup once emptied the
real cache, and states why one table is shared instead.
