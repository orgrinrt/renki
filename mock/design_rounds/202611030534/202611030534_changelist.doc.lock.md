# doc changelist

One sentence amended and one section added in renki's design, and the document
rendered from them. Descends from the topic file
`202611030534_topic.a-refused-build-forgets-its-branch-resolution.md`, the one
open topic, and is everything that topic asks for.

## What lands

- `mock/crates/renki/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, "Asking a remote for a branch head": the
  sentence saying a branch pin falls back to its last resolution gains its
  exception, a resolution forgotten because its build was refused, and points
  at the new section.
- `mock/crates/renki/DESIGN.md.tmpl`, a new section "A refused build forgets its
  resolution": a build that started and was refused removes the recorded
  resolution of its url and branch, so the next launch asks the remote; a build
  that could not start removes nothing. The two are told apart by a type. The
  one inexactness, cargo failing on a dropped network the way it fails on a
  broken revision, is named with its cost.
- `docs/`, rendered.

## Claims the source changelist will carry

- `BuildError` has `NotStarted` and `Refused`. `retry::install_with_retry`
  returns `Refused` when every attempt failed or built nothing, and
  `retry::cargo_is_on_path` and the failure to spawn cargo return `NotStarted`.
- `place` is generic over its error, `E: From<String>`, and
  `extension::materialise_once` still answers `String`. `Backend::materialise`
  keeps its text error and `Cargo`'s implementation turns a `BuildError` into
  its message.
- `cache::ensure_built` answers `BuildError`. A `Refused` out of it reaches
  `pin::forget_resolution` through `resolved_and_built`, and a `NotStarted`
  does not.
- `resolved_and_built` returns a named `Built`: the resolution, the toolchain,
  the key and the binary.
- `pin::forget_resolution` removes the recorded resolution of the pin's url and
  branch and no other, and a pin that names no branch has none to remove.
- The mutants of the new code are a table with its log, committed beside the
  round at `mock/research/202611030534_forget-resolution-mutants/`, each shown
  killed by a test.
