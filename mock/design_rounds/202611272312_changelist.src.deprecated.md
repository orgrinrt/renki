## Comparison to deprecated changelist

The deprecated changelist opened the source window for a `std`
`renki-manifest` and held no claims when the round was unlocked; the
`renki-config` half written under it (the layer, order, meta, merge, stack and
predicate modules, `Declared<'a, St>`, `Declared::named`, `Store::shape`,
`resolve` over two stacks) stands as it was and is claimed here unchanged.
What differs is everything that reads the disk: `renki-manifest` is
`#![no_std]` with no alloc over `libc`, reads into a caller's `Room`, and
parses with `tomlfuse-parse` instead of `toml_edit`; `renki` drops `toml` and
`toml_edit` and owns the one sizing retry for a caller with an allocator.

## Claims

- CHANGE `renki-config`: `layer_of`, `Part`, `LayerName`, `BadLayer`,
  `Order` with `Order::DEFAULT` and `Order::rank`, `Meta` with
  `Meta::defect`, `glob_matches`, `Shape`, `Meet`, `meet`, `BadMerge`,
  `Layer`, `stacked`, `ListRun`, `Predicate`, `Predicates`, `Answers`,
  `holds`, `truthy`, `BadPredicate`, `PredicateName`; `Declared<'a, St>` and
  `Declared::named`; `Store::shape`; `resolve` over two stacks; the layer in
  `ResolvedView` and `BadConfig`.
- CHANGE `renki-manifest`, `#![no_std]`, no alloc, on `renki-config`,
  `tomlfuse-parse`, `serde` and `libc` with default features off, feature
  `shell-predicate`, unix only, passing `no-std`, `no-alloc` and every
  `no-bare-*` lint with no exemption, allow-marker or ratchet: `read::<T>`,
  `Room`, `Host`, `Unix`, `BadRead`, `Manifest` with `find`, `view`, `read`,
  `stack`, `dump`, `Ambient`, and `Toml` over `tomlfuse-parse` writing at a
  key's span and under `[[name]]`.
- CHANGE `renki`: `config` reads through `renki-manifest`; `config get`
  prints the layer, `config path` the layers, `config set` and `config edit`
  take `--layer`; `config::toml` is removed; every TOML file goes through
  `renki-manifest`; `toml` and `toml_edit` leave its dependencies; the room
  is sized from `BadRead::Room` once, in `renki`, for every caller with an
  allocator.
- CHANGE `renki::extension::Descriptor` gains `who`, `moment`, `settings`;
  `extension::command` hands the resolved settings as `<NAME>_CFG_<KEY>`.
- CHANGE the tests the doc changelist lists, each red first, and the writer
  tests: a comment, an inline table, a dotted key, a multi-line string and an
  absent key, each compared with what `toml_edit` wrote for the same edit.
- CHANGE the C calls in one module of `renki-manifest`, each with a test on
  this host, and a build check per target `Unix` names.
