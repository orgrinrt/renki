# src changelist

Carries out `202611030534_changelist.doc.lock.md`. The one open topic,
`202611030534_topic.a-refused-build-forgets-its-branch-resolution.md`, is this
round's and is what everything below does.

## CHANGE: enum `retry::BuildError` FROM absent TO `NotStarted(String)` and `Refused(String)`
File: `mock/crates/renki/src/retry.rs`
Verification: `is_refused` is true for `Refused` alone, `into_message` answers
the text of either, a bare `String` becomes `NotStarted` through `From`, and
`From<BuildError> for String` answers the text. Tests:
`a_message_alone_is_a_build_that_never_started_and_reads_the_same_either_way`.
With `is_refused` made always false, always true, or `into_message` made empty,
and with `From<String>` made a `Refused`, the suite fails (mutants in the
table of the research directory, below).

## CHANGE: fn `retry::install_with_retry` FROM `Result<(), String>` TO `Result<(), BuildError>`
File: `mock/crates/renki/src/retry.rs`
Verification: every attempt failing, or succeeding and leaving no binary, is
`Refused`; a `run` that could not start cargo is `NotStarted` and stops
everything; an empty list of attempts is `NotStarted`, since nothing ran. Tests:
`two_failures_move_to_the_next_attempt_and_the_message_carries_the_retry` and
`a_missing_binary_is_named_as_that_rather_than_as_a_failure` assert
`is_refused`, `a_first_success_needs_no_pause_and_a_run_error_stops_everything`
asserts `NotStarted` of the run's text, and
`nothing_to_try_is_a_build_that_never_started_and_not_a_refusal` the empty list.
With the final `Refused` made `NotStarted`, and with the empty-list branch
taken away, each fails.

## CHANGE: fn `selfupdate::reinstall` FROM answering `install_with_retry`'s text TO reading the text out of a `BuildError`
File: `mock/crates/renki/src/selfupdate.rs`
Verification: the self-update has no branch resolution to forget and answers
the same text as before; the existing self-update tests pass unchanged.

## CHANGE: fn `extension::place` FROM answering `String` TO generic over its error, `E: From<String> + Into<String>`

Verification: `materialise_once` and `locate` instantiate it with `String` and

existing placement tests, with the closure of

## CHANGE: fn `extension::build_with_cargo` and fn `Cargo::build` FROM absent TO the launcher's own typed build
File: `mock/crates/renki/src/extension.rs`
Verification: `Cargo::build` is the body `Backend::materialise` had, answering a
`BuildError`; `Backend::materialise` for `Cargo` is `Cargo::build` read as its
text, so the public contract is as before. `build_with_cargo` is `place` with
`Cargo::places_itself()` and `Cargo::build`. Tests:

## CHANGE: fn `pin::forget_resolution` FROM absent TO removing the recorded resolution of a branch pin's url and branch
File: `mock/crates/renki/src/pin.rs`
Verification: it removes the file of that url and branch and no other, and a pin
that names no branch removes nothing. `branch_resolution_path` is `pub(crate)`
for the tests to read. Tests in `lib_tests/builds.rs`:
`a_refused_build_drops_its_branch_resolution_and_no_other_so_the_next_run_asks_the_remote`
checks the resolution of `dev` gone and `main` kept, and that the next
`pin::resolve` asks the remote and fails where it had answered from the record;
`a_refused_build_of_a_pin_that_names_no_branch_leaves_a_branchs_resolution_alone`
the rev pin. With the removal taken out, and with it aimed at another branch or
another remote, the suite fails.

## CHANGE: fn `resolved_and_built` and struct `Built` FROM the inline resolve, key and build of `dispatch` TO a function returning a named `Built`
File: `mock/crates/renki/src/lib.rs`
Verification: `Built` holds the resolution, the toolchain, the key and the
binary, and `dispatch` takes them out by name. A `Refused` out of the build
reaches `pin::forget_resolution` and its text comes back; a `NotStarted` forgets
nothing and its text comes back; a build that worked forgets nothing. Tests:
`a_build_that_could_not_start_keeps_the_resolution_it_says_nothing_about` is the
control of the first, and
`a_build_that_succeeds_keeps_the_resolution_and_hands_back_what_it_built` checks
the key against `cache::compute_key` of the resolution and toolchain. The
rebuild after an eviction in `dispatch` reads its failure as text and forgets
nothing, since a revision that built before and is now refused is a moved
toolchain or machine and not a bad revision. With the forget taken out, made
unconditional, or made to follow a build that worked, the suite fails.

## What the suite is worth

The tests reach the decision (what a failure forgets), its two controls (a
build that could not start, a build that worked), the scope of the forget (its
own url and branch and no other, a pin with no branch), and the typed failure
read off a real cargo. What none of them reaches is a network that drops under
a cargo download and so reads as a refusal: from here it cannot be told from a
broken revision, which the design says and which costs one `ls-remote`.
