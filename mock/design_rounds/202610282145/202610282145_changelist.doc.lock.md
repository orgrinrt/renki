# doc changelist

The design documents of the three crates, amended to say what the clearing of
ten lint findings makes true: six public types renamed so no two crates define
one name, the placement property held by a lint of the repository's own, the
build-per-key design the branch already implements written down, and a ledger
for the two ignored tests. Descends from the topic file
`202610282145_topic.the-one-engine-install-branch-carries-ten-lint-findings.md`,
which is the only open topic and is consumed whole.

## What lands

- `mock/crates/renki-dirs/DESIGN.md.tmpl`: `Kind` is `DirKind`, `Source` is
  `PathSource`, and the private `Sealed` is `DirsSealed`.
- `mock/crates/renki-config/DESIGN.md.tmpl`: `Source` is `Origin`, `Resolved`
  is `ResolvedView`, and the private supertrait closing `Scope` is named,
  `ConfigSealed`. The prose that said a value's "source" says its origin, so
  the word has one meaning left in the crate. `Kind` keeps its name here, being
  the only one of the two once the other is renamed.
- `mock/crates/renki/DESIGN.md.tmpl`: two sections. "One build per key" says
  the key, the lock held across the build, the second lock over the shared
  target directory and the order of the two, the toolchain answering in the
  repository's root, and the absolute cache root. "Placement is decided once"
  says that `place` is the one body deciding how a backend's material is placed
  and names the lint that holds it. The `Source` and `Resolved` of this crate
  keep their names.
- `mock/crates/renki/LEDGER.md`: new, two rows, `#30` and `#31`, the issues the
  two ignored tests in `retry.rs` stand for.
- `docs/CONFIG.md`, `docs/DIRS.md`, `docs/RENKI.md`,
  `docs/STRUCTURE.GRAPH.png`, `docs/STRUCTURE.GRAPH.svg` and `.gitattributes`,
  rendered.

The `Kind` column of the table in `mock/crates/renki-dirs/README.md` names the
four directory kinds as a word and not the trait, and the root readme's
`Resolved` is this crate's own pin type, which keeps its name. Neither changes.

## Claims the source changelist will carry

The six renames at every site that names them, in `renki-dirs`, `renki-config`
and `renki`, with the tests and doctests that spell them; the two
`; tracked #30` and `; tracked #31` on the ignored tests; the one helper that
holds a second launcher on a thread for the two lock tests, carrying the
`no-runtime-spawn` allow and its reason; the lint
`mock/lints/the_placement_branch_exists_once.rs` with its tests and the
deletion of `nothing_but_place_decides_how_material_is_placed`; and the
`[lints.catalogue-ignore]` table in `mockspace.toml` naming the ledger. Each is
a `CHANGE` block there with its own verification.

## Not changed, and why

The closed rounds and their topics, which are archives. The rendered
`docs/STRUCTURE.md` lists the types as the source declares them, so it
regenerates once more in the source window, when the names in it move.
